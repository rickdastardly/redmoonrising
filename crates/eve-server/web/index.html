                  Bot population

                  Number of bots

                  Loading bot settings…

                  Bots are created on the first start that needs them and kept after; a lower number plays fewer of them. Saved to rmr.yml.

                  Aggression

                  Bots always fight back when a player shoots them. Calm bots also drop any player they were chasing.

                  Pirates attack on sight (with "some")
                  Gangs and fleets

                  Bot alliances send gangs roaming low and null-sec, a few systems at a time for a couple of hours. Roaming gangs attack players outside high-sec on sight. See the gangs out now, or send one, on the Events, raids and gangs page.

                  Roaming gangs
                  Gangs hunt for players
                  Bots join your gang when invited

                  Invite an armed bot ship in your system to your gang: it flies on your wing, follows you through gates and shoots what shoots you or what you lock. Up to five at once. The hourly rate runs from 0.5m ISK for a frigate to 10m for a battleship.

                  Sovereignty warfare

                  How hard bot alliances fight over null-sec. Turn it down if sieges and battles start to weigh on the server. See the sieges and battles on the Sieges and battles page.

                  Sov warfare

                  Battle size (ships a side)

                  The most ships each side brings to a battle, its allies included. A side brings fewer when the stakes are low or it hasn't the pilots. Takes effect from the next battle. How many you see on grid is still capped by "Bot ships shown per system" below. The rest fight as reserves coming in and in the background.

                  Mining and the market
                  Mining fleets
                  Bots mine belts out

                  Miners from the same station work the same belt as a fleet, each on its own asteroid. With belts mined out, the rocks they work shrink and vanish where you can see them.

                  Bot traders' orders

                  Trader bots keep sell and buy orders at the busiest hubs and wherever you are docked, for everything the NPC market there sells: ships, modules, ammunition, drones, implants, minerals and ore. Prices sit around the market's, a little over or under. Orders stand for one to three days and are replaced every five minutes or so.

                  Local chat

                  How much bots talk in Local: among themselves, back to you, and after kills.

                  Trash talk and trolling
                  Corporations and alliances
                  Bots apply to your corporations
                  Bot corporations apply to your alliances
                  Bot corporations and alliances answer your applications

                  Bot corporations and alliances answer within about ten minutes; an accepted alliance membership starts a day later, as in EVE.

                  Activity

                  How many bots are logged in, compared with normal. They log on or off over the next few minutes; at most 97% can be online at once.

                  The most bot ships in space in one system where a player is, up to 5000 (a 2,000 against 2,000 battle needs 4,000 or more). More means bigger fights and far more work for the server and your client.

                  Capital battle

                  A battle fought at once, with a titan, dreadnoughts, carriers, logistics and a cyno ship on both sides. Name any system: where a bot alliance has a claiming tower it is a siege round that tower; anywhere else the fleets meet in open space, round a beacon about 45 km from you, and the dreadnoughts fire on each other. Leave it blank for a siege wherever a bot alliance holds a tower; the news and the server log say where. Be in space there to watch it: the cyno, the capitals jumping in, the titan's bridge, siege mode, bombardment, repairs and doomsdays. Same as /bots battle in game.

                  Events and Controls

                  Station market seeding

                  Stock a station's market with NPC sell orders for every marketable item, priced like the regional hubs. Seeded stations are topped back up at every server start. With an empty search the list shows player-owned stations (outposts) and stations already seeded.

                  Mission & DED Complex Guides

                  A walkthrough for every agent mission and DED complex on the server, written from the same data the server spawns them from: each pocket, its gates, every wave and what brings it, the overseers and the structures. Open a guide to read it.

                  Deadspace Complexes

                  Give an online pilot a DED complex to test, in their own system or any system you name. If they are in space in that system, it opens about 0.03 AU from them and their ship warps straight there. Otherwise it is waiting for them in their System Scanner as a result already pinned down, with no probes needed: right-click it and choose Warp To. In the complex, right-click each acceleration gate and choose Activate Gate to move on; a locked gate opens once that room's defenders are dead.

                  GM Commands

                  Type them in any chat channel

                  These slash commands work for accounts with the ISD, BH, GM or CCP role. Give an account a role on the User accounts page; the player gets it the next time they log in. Types, skills and places can be given by name or by ID. Words in <angle brackets> are required, [square brackets] optional.

                  CommandWhat it doesExample
                  Items and ships
                  /create <type> [quantity]Creates items in your station hangar, or your cargo hold in space. The type is an ID or a name./create Raven 1
                  /gmshipsOne of every staff, Jovian and test hull, in your station hangar./gmships
                  /outpostmats [platformID]Puts the materials an outpost platform needs into it (the nearest one if no ID is given)./outpostmats
                  /rushanchor [structureID]Finishes anchoring or onlining a starbase structure straight away./rushanchor
                  Character
                  /giveisk <amount>Adds ISK to your wallet. Also /givemoney./giveisk 1000000000
                  /giveskill <skill> [level]Trains one skill straight into your character (level 5 if no level is given)./giveskill Caldari Battleship 5
                  /giveallskillsEvery skill at level 5./giveallskills
                  /setstanding <ownerID> <standing>Sets an agent's, NPC corporation's or faction's standing towards you (-10 to 10)./setstanding 500001 10
                  /maxstandingsMaximum standing from every agent, NPC corporation and faction. Your Standings window updates at once./maxstandings
                  Travel
                  /tr me <place or pilot>Teleports you to a station, solar system, celestial or another pilot./tr me Jita
                  /moveme [hub]Moves you to a trade hub (Jita, Amarr, Rens, Dodixie, Hek) or a named null-sec station. With no hub, shows a picker./moveme jita
                  Space
                  /spawnSpawns a group of the system's rats around your ship./spawn
                  /clearspaceRemoves every jettisoned can and cargo container in space, with its contents./clearspace
                  Bots
                  /bots on | offStarts or pauses the simulated pilots./bots off
                  /bots statusHow many bots are online, in space and doing what./bots status
                  /bots event roamSends a bot alliance's gang of 5 to 12 fighters roaming low and null-sec for about two hours. With hunting on (Bots page), it heads for systems where players are in space and attacks them outside high-sec./bots event roam
                  /bots event raidSends a pirate fleet to raid a low-sec system near its space for two hours. Raiders attack players there on sight. Bots never start fights in high-sec./bots event raid
                  /bots event <kind>Starts an event: betrayal, merger, collapse, coalition, raid, or roam (a roaming gang)./bots event raid
                  /bots war <a> | <b>Declares war between two bot alliances or corporations./bots war Goonswarm | Band of Brothers
                  /bots density <region> <factor>Sets how busy a region is with bots./bots density Delve 2
                  /bots battle [here | system] [skirmish | battle | decisive]Starts a battle at once, with every kind of capital ship on both sides: a titan, dreadnoughts, carriers, logistics and a cyno ship. here is your own system. Where a bot alliance has a claiming tower it is a siege round it; anywhere else the fleets meet in open space by you (be in space to see it). With no system, a siege wherever a bot alliance holds a tower. Decisive unless you say otherwise. Also on the Bots page, in the Capital battle window./bots battle here
                  /bots follow <name>Follows a pilot on the dashboard's Pilot tracking page. /bots unfollow <characterID> stops./bots follow Rick
